About us

A technology company, headquartered in Florida.

MacLorian X Group LLC was founded in March 2026 in the state of Florida. We focus on the development, management, and operation of mobile applications, digital platforms, and technology services.

Our focus is on building useful products for the entire world, with SW Florida as our pilot market. Each division operates as an independent business unit with its own market and metrics, under the corporate umbrella of the holding.
